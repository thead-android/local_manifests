## Acceptance evidence

- Three controlled trials issuing touch startup while the display slept failed
  after wake, despite I2C ACKs. The matched after-wake trials and no-command
  sleep/wake controls passed. Thus an ACK alone is not startup completion.
- The final module passed two warm boots and five display OFF/ON cycles.
- The cycle trace contains exactly five SENSE_OFF and five SENSE_ON commands,
  correct firmware state transitions, no lost trace entries and no I2C errors.
- A five-second quiet window has zero I2C traffic: no hidden background poller.
- At 138 seconds after the second boot, Android boot was complete and an
  explicit read still showed active scanning, with IRQ enabled and only one
  startup. SELinux remained Enforcing and CFI was retained.
- The owner accepted this automated evidence without a final manual touch
  test. Do not claim final physical point/drag or cold-power-cycle validation.

The periodic-recovery experiment, one-check-only experiment, and completion-
timeout-only variant are excluded. Timing observations do not prove the exact
internal firmware mechanism. Full-system suspend still has a separate hang;
display-cycle tests used temporary suspend blockers and released them afterward.
